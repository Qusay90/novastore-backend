# Code of Conduct

NovaStore welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints. Everyone taking part in project spaces is expected to help keep them constructive and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and work in good faith; give feedback that is specific and respectful.
- Accept review and disagreement without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish credentials, personal information, customer data, or private correspondence.
- Use synthetic data and authorized environments when reproducing problems.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Insults, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, or deliberate disruption of project discussion.
- Publishing private information, secrets, or customer data.
- Testing against production services or third-party providers without explicit authorization.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces controlled by the maintainers.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, decline contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project community. They will consider the context and impact of a report and communicate a decision where practical.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer through the [Qusay90 GitHub profile](https://github.com/Qusay90) and request a private channel. Do not include sensitive or personal information in a public issue. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
